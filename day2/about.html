About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple place to capture, review, and remember your ideas.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the QuickNotes home page.
  2. Type an idea into the new note field.
  3. Select Add Note to save it to your notes list.

Features

  • Quick note capture
  • Easy-to-scan notes list
  • Simple keyboard-friendly controls
